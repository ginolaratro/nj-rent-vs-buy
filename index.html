A NOTE ON THE NUMBERS

What goes into the math?

Buying includes your down payment, closing costs, mortgage payments, property tax, insurance, maintenance, HOA fees, and selling costs, less the proceeds when you sell. Renting includes rent and renters insurance. The model invests the initial cash needed to buy and the monthly difference between renting and buying at your chosen return. It assumes monthly compounding and yearly increases in rent, home value, taxes, insurance, and maintenance.

PMI is estimated on the original loan and stops when the scheduled balance reaches 80% of the original home price. Actual cancellation depends on your loan and servicer. The model excludes income taxes, tax deductions, rent deposits, moving costs, and transaction-specific fees. Negative invested balances represent borrowing at the same assumed return; this is a simplifying assumption.
